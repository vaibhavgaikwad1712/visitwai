Best time to go

October to February is the easiest season for every package. In other months we adjust the stops.

MonthsWhat to expect
Oct–FebClear views, the best sunrises and sunsets, paragliding open
Dec–AprStrawberry picking at Bhilar, Avkali and Gureghar; Mapro Strawberry Festival in spring
Mar–MayWarmer days and busy summer holidays; book early
Jun–SepHeavy rain and waterfalls at their best; paragliding stops, some points close, boating may pause, fog can hide views
Aug–OctKaas Plateau flowers, available as an add-on

Good to know: sunrise moves between about 6:10 am and 7:10 am through the year, so we set sunrise pickup times by the month. In monsoon we swap closed points for waterfalls such as Lingmala, Chinaman's Falls, Bhilar Falls and Borgaon.
