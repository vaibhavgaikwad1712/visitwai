Terms & Conditions

A good trip needs respect on both sides. Here's what our drivers promise you, and what we ask of every traveller.

What your driver promises

Every Visit Wai driver agrees to these rules for the whole trip.

  1. No drinking or smoking

    Your driver will not drink alcohol or smoke while on duty, inside or outside the vehicle.

  2. Dual language support

    Your driver can talk with you in Marathi and Hindi.

  3. Photos are a favour, not a service

    Your driver may help take your photos when he is free, but this is entirely his choice. Please don't insist or force him.

  4. No nuisance

    Your driver will be polite and respectful, and will not create any nuisance or disturb your trip.

  5. Help in an emergency

    If anything goes wrong, your driver will help you: reaching the nearest hospital, clinic or police station, and informing the Visit Wai coordinator.

  6. Safe driving

    Your driver will drive carefully on the ghat roads, follow traffic rules and keep to safe speeds.

  7. Follows your itinerary

    Your driver will follow the agreed plan and will not take you to shops or stops you didn't ask for.

Code of conduct for travellers

By booking with Visit Wai, you agree to the following.

  1. Be on time

    Please be ready at the agreed pickup and meeting times. If points are missed because of delays caused by travellers, Visit Wai is not responsible. Your driver may sometimes need to finish on time and return home, so how much is covered depends on your coordination with the driver.

  2. Respect your driver

    No abusive language, shouting, threats or physical misbehaviour towards the driver. Any complaint or charge about the trip must be raised with Visit Wai, not settled with the driver.

  3. Tipping is your choice

    Tips are never required. Your driver will not ask or force you to tip.

  4. Do not damage property

    Travellers, including children, must not damage the vehicle, hotel rooms, monuments, temples or any public or private property. Parents and guardians are responsible for their children. The cost of any damage will be charged to the person responsible.

  5. No alcohol, smoking or drugs in the vehicle

    Drinking, smoking and drugs are not allowed inside the car. Illegal items are not allowed on the trip at all.

  6. Keep the car clean

    Please don't leave litter or spill food and drinks in the vehicle. Cleaning charges may apply if the car is badly soiled.

  7. Stay safe at viewpoints

    Do not cross railings, climb barriers or go near cliff edges for photos. Follow your driver's advice and local safety signs, especially in monsoon.

  8. Keep the hills clean

    Do not litter at viewpoints, lakes, forts or villages. Please use dustbins or carry your waste back to the car.

  9. Respect temples and local customs

    Dress modestly at temples and heritage sites, and follow their rules on footwear, photography and timings.

  10. Carry valid ID

    Every adult must carry a valid government photo ID. Hotels need it at check-in, and couples are welcome only with valid ID.

  11. Look after your belongings

    Visit Wai and the driver are not responsible for loss of or damage to personal belongings, cash or valuables.

  12. Changes to the plan

    Extra stops, extra time or extra distance you request are subject to availability and may be charged extra. Agree on any change with Visit Wai before or during the trip.

Cancellation & refund policy

We book your car, driver and hotel in advance for your dates, so refunds depend on how early you cancel. The days are counted from the date we receive your cancellation email.

When you cancelRefund of the amount paid
8 or more days before the trip start date75% refund
4 to 7 days before the trip start date50% refund
0 to 3 days before the trip start dateNo refund
No show on the day of pickupNo refund
  1. Refund timing

    Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment account within 5–7 working days.

  2. Changing the traveller, not the date

    Instead of cancelling, you can pass your booking to another person on the same dates. Bookings cannot be moved to a different date as a replacement for cancelling.

  3. If we cancel before the trip starts

    If Visit Wai cancels before your trip begins, for example because of heavy rain, landslides, government orders or a problem on our side, you get a full refund of the amount paid.

  4. Once the trip has started

    If weather, closures by authorities, landslides or local restrictions stop some points or activities after the trip has begun, we will offer the nearest feasible alternative, but no refund is given for the missed points.

  5. Leaving early or unused services

    If you end the trip early, or skip a stay, meal or point that was included, no refund is given for the unused part.

  6. Traffic and vehicle breakdowns

    Ghat roads can have traffic jams, and vehicles can break down. If a car breaks down, we will arrange a replacement as quickly as possible. Delays from traffic or breakdowns are not refunded.

  7. Onward trains, buses and flights

    Please keep a buffer between your drop time and any onward train, bus or flight. If a delay makes you miss a connection, we will help you find alternatives, but the cost of new tickets or extra stays is paid by you, and Visit Wai does not pay compensation.

  8. Activities paid to operators

    Boating, paragliding, amusement parks and other activities you pay for directly follow the operator's own cancellation rules.

How to cancel

  1. Email us

    Send an email to with your name, phone number, package and trip date. Only cancellations sent by email are accepted, so that there is a clear written record. Calls and WhatsApp messages are not treated as cancellations.

  2. Confirmation

    We reply within 24 hours to confirm your cancellation and the refund amount, if any. The policy above applies from the time we receive your email.

  3. No refund due?

    If your cancellation is not eligible for a refund under this policy, please do not request a refund or a date change.

Prices, add-ons and driver-only bookings

  1. How prices work

    Package prices are a per-day package cost plus the hotel you choose for each night of the stay. Optional add-ons are charged per day. The price shown in the booking form is based on your selections; we confirm availability and the final booking with you by phone.

  2. Photographer add-on

    A photographer accompanies you for the booked days and takes DSLR photos of your trip. Photo opportunities depend on weather, light and how the day goes.

  3. Drone add-on

    Up to 10 drone shots per booked day. Drones are flown only where the law and local authorities allow, and never near restricted areas, crowds or places where flying is prohibited. Rain, fog, strong wind or local restrictions can stop drone flights on the day; the drone pilot's decision on safety is final.

  4. Driver-only bookings

    Our driver drives your own vehicle at the per-day rate. The vehicle must have valid registration, insurance and PUC papers. Fuel, tolls, parking and any fines or damage linked to the vehicle's condition are the owner's responsibility. You don't need to pay for the driver's food unless you choose to.

  5. Custom trips

    Custom trips are priced on request. We send you a plan and price, and the booking is confirmed only once you accept it.

Booking terms

  1. Accepting these terms

    Every enquiry and booking requires you to accept these Terms & Conditions and Code of Conduct. Enquiries sent without accepting them are not processed.

  2. Couple packages for one person

    If only one person travels on a couple package, the same couple price applies, because the car, driver and room are the same.

  3. Weather, closures and traffic

    Points, waterfalls, boating and paragliding can close because of weather, local authorities or operators, and ghat traffic can delay the plan. We replace closed points with nearby open ones where possible.

  4. Activities paid at the venue

    Boating, rides, paragliding, amusement parks and other activities not listed as included are paid by travellers directly to the operators. Their prices and rules are set by them.

  5. Itinerary timings

    Timings on our itineraries are approximate. Your driver and coordinator may adjust the order of stops on the day for traffic, weather or crowds.

Questions about these terms? Email or WhatsApp us before you book.
