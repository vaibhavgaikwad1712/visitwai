How we keep the trip safe

Most of our guests are first-time visitors to the hills. These are the things we do on every trip.

Known drivers

Local drivers who know the ghat roads. You get the driver's name, photo and car number before pickup.

Checked hotels

We only book hotels we have visited, with clean rooms, CCTV at entry and staff on duty at night.

One number to call

A Visit Wai coordinator is reachable on phone and WhatsApp for the whole trip.

Your privacy

Couples are welcome with valid ID. Your details are never shared outside the booking.
