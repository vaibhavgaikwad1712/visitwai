What we cover in 2 days

Timings are approximate and adjust to traffic, weather and your pace.

Day 1 Wai → Panchgani → Mahabaleshwar

  1. 8:00 amPickup in Wai

    From your home, bus stand or hotel. Quick darshan at Dholya Ganpati on the Krishna ghats.

  2. 9:00 amPasarni Ghat drive

    Photo stop on the ghat road with views over the Wai valley.

  3. 10:00 amTable Land, Panchgani

    The flat volcanic plateau. Horse rides and snacks available.

  4. 11:30 amSydney Point & Parsi Point

    Views of the Krishna valley and Dhom dam backwaters.

  5. 1:00 pmLunch
  6. 2:30 pmMapro Garden & strawberry farm

    Strawberries with cream and farm visits (fresh berries Nov–Apr).

  7. 4:00 pmHotel check-in, Mahabaleshwar
  8. 5:30 pmVenna Lake & market

    Boating at sunset, then the main market for chikki, fudge and jams.

  9. 8:30 pmDinner and night stay

Day 2 Mahabaleshwar points → back to Wai

  1. 6:15 amSunrise at Wilson Point

    Optional early start. The highest point in Mahabaleshwar.

  2. 8:30 amBreakfast & check-out
  3. 9:30 amArthur's Seat & Echo Point

    Deep valley views towards the Savitri river and Konkan.

  4. 11:00 amElephant's Head & Needle Hole Point
  5. 12:00 pmOld Mahabaleshwar temples

    Panchganga temple, source of the Krishna, and Mahabaleshwar Shiva temple.

  6. 1:30 pmLunch
  7. 3:00 pmLingmala Waterfall

    Best in and after the monsoon (Jul–Oct).

  8. 4:30 pmKate's Point on the way down

    Views over Dhom and Balkawadi dams.

  9. 6:30 pmDrop in Wai

    Drop to Satara or Pune on request at extra cost.

For the senior package, we skip Wilson Point sunrise and Needle Hole Point, and add longer rest breaks. Some points close during heavy monsoon; we replace them with nearby open spots.

What food is served

Meals are optional. Add them while booking, or eat on your own. Veg, non-veg and Jain options available.

Breakfast

Poha, upma, misal pav, thalipeeth with white butter, bread-omelette, tea and coffee.

Lunch

Maharashtrian veg thali with bhakri, pithla, varan-bhaat and koshimbir. Chicken or mutton thali for non-veg.

Evening snacks

Roasted corn, strawberries with cream, Mapro shakes, vada pav and hot chai at the points.

Dinner

Hotel dinner: chapati, paneer or seasonal sabzi, dal, rice and sweet. Chicken curry for non-veg.

For seniors

Low-spice, low-oil meals, khichdi and soft food, diabetic-friendly options, warm water on request.
