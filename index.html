    What's included

    Sightseeing entry is part of the package price. Activities you choose on the day are paid at the venue. See approximate activity prices.

    Included in every package

    • AC car with driver, fuel, tolls and driver allowance
    • Parking at all points and temples
    • Municipal tourist tax at Panchgani and Mahabaleshwar
    • Budget hotel stay (worth ₹1,000 per room per night), with upgrades available
    • Entry tickets for viewpoints, temples and heritage sites on the itinerary
    • History guide on Ancient Wai

    Not included

    • Boating, horse, pony and camel rides
    • Paragliding, go-karting and amusement parks
    • Strawberry picking and craft workshops
    • Meals, unless you add the food option
    • Photographer, drone shots and caretaker, unless you add them
    • Camera fees, shopping and tips

    How we keep the trip safe

    Most of our guests are first-time visitors to the hills. These are the things we do on every trip.

    Known drivers

    Local drivers who know the ghat roads. You get the driver's name, photo and car number before pickup.

    Checked hotels

    We only book hotels we have visited, with clean rooms, CCTV at entry and staff on duty at night.

    One number to call

    A Visit Wai coordinator is reachable on phone and WhatsApp for the whole trip.

    Your privacy

    Couples are welcome with valid ID. Your details are never shared outside the booking.
