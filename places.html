Places to visit

Temples, ghats, viewpoints, waterfalls, farms, museums and adventure parks across Wai, Panchgani and Mahabaleshwar. Our packages cover most of them.

Wai Panchgani Mahabaleshwar Adventure Day trips Experiences

Wai

Known as "Dakshin Kashi" for its temples on the Krishna river ghats. See it in depth on our Ancient Wai package.

Temples and ghats

Dholya (Maha) Ganpati Mandir

The big stone Ganpati on the Krishna ghat and Wai's main landmark.

Kashi Vishweshwar Mandir

Beside the Ganpati temple on the same ghat.

The seven ghats of Wai

Brahmanshahi ghat has four temples (Chakreshwar, Chimneshwar, Kaunteshwar/Harihareshwar and Kaleshwar). Ramdoh ghat has the Rameshwar temple and Ramkund. Gangapuri ghat has Shiva, Dwarka, Bahiroba and Dattatray temples.

Menavali Ghat

Built by Nana Phadnavis, with Hemadpanti-style Vishnu and Meneshwar temples. Films like "Swades" and "Gangaajal" were shot here. Look for the large bell brought back from the Portuguese after Chimaji Appa's victory at Vasai.

Narsimha and Dhomeshwar temples

Near Dhom Dam.

Mandhardev Kalubai temple

A major hilltop pilgrimage temple near Wai, very crowded during the yatra in Jan–Feb.

Heritage

Nana Phadnavis Wada, Menavali

Six courtyards and teak-wood lattice corridors. Interiors are usually open on weekends. Name souvenirs in Modi lipi are sold here.

Wai Caves (Lohare)

Nine Buddhist caves, 7 km north of Wai.

Dams and nature

Dhom Dam and Dhom Lake

Boating, picnics and sunsets.

Balkawadi Dam

Behind Dhom, best seen from Kate's Point.

Borgaon waterfalls

Four waterfalls between Dhom and Balkawadi dams, about 16 km from Wai (monsoon only).

Pasarni Ghat

The ghat road up to Panchgani, with valley viewpoints.

Forts, treks and festivals

Forts around Wai

Kamalgad, Pandavgad, Vairatgad, Kenjalgad and Chandan–Vandan. Pandavgad and Kamalgad are the most popular treks.

Krishnamai Utsav

Wai's main festival, celebrated on each ghat for four to eight days.

Panchgani

Plateaus, boarding-school heritage and two unique villages: one full of books, one full of artisans.

Points

Table Land

A laterite plateau about 4.5 km long, said to be the second longest mountain plateau in Asia. Horse rides, pony carts, go-karting and food stalls.

Devil's Kitchen

A cave at the southern end of Table Land where the Pandavas are believed to have stayed.

Sydney Point

Views of the Krishna valley and Dhom Dam.

Parsi Point

Valley and Dhom backwater views on the Mahabaleshwar road.

Harrison's Folly

A flat clifftop on the Wai–Panchgani road with paragliding, camel rides and horse rides.

Culture and villages

Devrai Art Village

Adivasi craftsmen from Gadchiroli and Chhattisgarh make brass, wood, iron, bamboo, fabric and stone art. Visitors can join workshops.

Bhilar Book Village

India's first "village of books": more than 35,000 books to read free at 35 homes, schools, lodges and temples. Open 9 am to 6 pm. Also a strawberry village.

Rajpuri Caves

7 km from Panchgani, known for the Swami Karthikeya temple and its holy ponds.

St. Peter's Church

A Gothic-style church from the late 19th century.

Old Panchgani

Colonial boarding schools and heritage bungalows, best on foot.

Panchgani market (Shivaji Circle)

Chikki, fudge, jams and local shopping.

Nature

Bhilar Falls

On the Mahabaleshwar–Panchgani road (monsoon).

Nagewadi Dam

A quiet reservoir in the hills near Panchgani.

Sherbaug

A garden park with animals and a kids' area.

Mahabaleshwar

Over 20 viewpoints, the source of the Krishna, strawberry farms and Venna Lake.

Viewpoints

  • Wilson Point, the highest point, best for sunrise
  • Arthur's Seat, with Window Point, Tiger Spring and Echo Point
  • Kate's Point
  • Needle Hole and Elephant's Head Point
  • Bombay Point, for sunset
  • Connaught Peak, views of Panchgani, Pasarni and Pratapgad
  • Lodwick Point
  • Elphinstone Point
  • Marjorie Point
  • Savitri Point
  • Castle Rock
  • Babington Point
  • Carnac Point
  • Falkland Point
  • Hunter's Point and Gavlani Point
  • Malcolm Point and Helen's Point

Waterfalls

  • Lingmala Waterfall, best Jul–Oct
  • Chinaman's Falls
  • Dhobi Waterfall

Temples (Old Mahabaleshwar)

Mahabaleshwar Shiva Temple

The ancient temple the town is named after.

Panchganga Mandir

The source of five rivers, including the Krishna.

Krishnabai Temple

Old ruins with valley views.

Atibaleshwar Temple

Close to the Mahabaleshwar temple.

Lake, gardens and farms

Venna Lake

Boating, horse rides, and stalls with corn and strawberries.

Mapro Garden (Gureghar)

Chocolate factory, restaurant, nursery, kids' play area and shop. Hosts the Strawberry Festival every spring.

Strawberry farms

Pick your own at farms like Laxmi Strawberry Farm, and in Avkali, Bhilar and Gureghar (Dec–Apr).

Honey centre

Mahabaleshwar honey from the Khadi Board outlet.

Museums and heritage

Wax Museum (Moleshwar village)

Wax sculptures made by visually impaired artisans.

Colonial Mahabaleshwar

Morarji Castle, Christ Church and old colonial bungalows.

Main market

Chikki, fudge, jams, chappals and leather goods.

Adventure parks and activities

Activities are paid at the venue. See approximate prices.

Paragliding

Tandem flights at Panchgani Table Land and Harrison's Folly (Oct–May).

Velocity Entertainmentz

Adventure park at Bhose village on the Panchgani–Mahabaleshwar road: go-karting, zip-lining, ATV rides, rope courses and rappelling.

On Wheelz Amusement Park

Near Sanjeevan School, Taighat, Panchgani, with rides like Tsunami, Octopus, Free-fall and Zyclone.

Funtown Amusement Park

On the Panchgani–Mahabaleshwar road.

Dhandeghar amusement park

On the Wai–Panchgani road, with gardens, waterfalls and a horror house.

On the water

Boating at Venna Lake and Dhom Dam, water sports at Dhom, kayaking at Tapola and Bamnoli.

Treks

Pandavgad, Kamalgad and Pratapgad forts.

Camping

Dhom backwaters, Tapola and Bamnoli.

Riding, cycling and walks

Horse riding at Table Land and Venna Lake; cycling and nature walks around Panchgani.

Nearby day trips

Add any of these to a package. Ask us when you enquire.

Pratapgad Fort

Site of Shivaji Maharaj's encounter with Afzal Khan, about 24 km from Mahabaleshwar.

Tapola ("Mini Kashmir")

Shivsagar lake with boating and camping.

Bamnoli

Boating and camping on the Koyna backwaters.

Kaas Plateau

The "Valley of Flowers", in bloom roughly Aug–Oct. Entry is booked online.

Thoseghar Waterfall

Near Satara, monsoon only.

Experiences not to miss

  • Strawberries with cream, fresh from the farm (Dec–Apr)
  • Sunrise at Wilson Point and sunset at Bombay Point
  • Mapro's Strawberry Festival and chocolate factory
  • Reading under trees at Bhilar Book Village
  • A craft workshop at Devrai Art Village
  • Paragliding over the Krishna valley
  • Roasted corn and chai in the monsoon mist
  • Monsoon waterfalls: Lingmala, Chinaman's, Bhilar, Borgaon
  • The Menavali ghat film location and Nana Phadnavis Wada
  • An evening on the Wai ghats during Krishnamai Utsav
  • Chikki, fudge, jam and honey shopping

Some points and waterfalls close in heavy monsoon, and opening days and park timings change. We check before your trip and plan around closures.
